Design Decision 01 · Beyond Chat Boxes

Chat forces high cognitive friction for continuous parameters. Tactile Intent Dials let users shape reasoning depth, risk leash, and density with game-HUD immediacy before and during execution.

Design Decision 02 · Lossless Mid-Flight Brake

Traditional agents force a binary "Stop & Lose Progress" vs "Wait Helplessly". Pressing Space applies kinetic deceleration at the active step boundary so users inject constraints and fork cleanly.

Design Decision 03 · Shared Ownership & Audio Cues

Synthesized Web Audio micro-feedback (intent lock chord, brake down-sweep, takeover chime) pairs with color-coded borders (Cyan = Agent, Amber = Braked, Purple = Human Control) so agency state is unmistakable in peripheral vision.
